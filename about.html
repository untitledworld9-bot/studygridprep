About Study Grid Prep

Study Grid Prep is a student-focused digital platform built to help students study smarter, stay organized, and access the information that actually matters to them — all from one place. What started as a simple exam-prep tool has grown into a broader platform that supports students through their everyday academic life, not just their exams.

What Study Grid Prep Offers

The platform brings together practical study tools designed for daily use:

  • Focus Timer to help you study with intent and track your sessions
  • Todo List to plan and stay on top of your daily tasks
  • Study & YouTube Playlists to organize your learning material
  • Progress Tracking to see how consistent you've been over time
  • Mock Tests and other academic tools to help you prepare and practice

Content Hub

Alongside these tools, Study Grid Prep now includes a dedicated Content Hub — a single place to stay updated and informed as a student. It brings together:

  • Educational updates
  • Latest student and news updates
  • College information
  • Notes and study resources
  • Blogs and articles
  • Career and academic information
  • Other useful resources for students

Study Grid Prep is built for students preparing for JEE, NEET, CUET, board exams, and beyond — anyone looking for a more focused and organized way to study, along with the information they need to make better academic decisions.
