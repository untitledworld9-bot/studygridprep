About Study Grid Prep

Start here to understand what the platform is and what it covers.

Study Grid Prep is a study platform for students preparing for JEE Main, NEET, CUET UG and Board Exams. It brings together mock tests, AI-powered performance analysis, a Smart Focus Timer, live study rooms, a Study Playlist, AI doubt solving, AI quiz generation, a Todo Planner, progress tracking, leaderboard features and a Content Hub.

Study Grid Prep is designed around JEE Main, NEET, CUET UG and Board Exam preparation, with mock tests and study tools tailored for these student needs.

Content Hub brings study resources such as notes, PYQs, exam updates, college information and blogs into one place. It is designed to make useful student resources easier to discover.

Yes. The Study Grid Prep Blog contains study-related insights, guides, updates and useful resources. You can browse the blog and use its search and filtering interface when available.

Study Grid Prep is primarily a study-tool platform that supports self-study. It focuses on practice, analysis, focus, planning and study resources rather than replacing classroom or coaching instruction.

Mock Tests & AI Study Tools

Questions about practice, analysis, AI assistance, quizzes and focus tools.

Mock tests are designed for competitive-exam practice and can include full-length and subject-focused practice. The platform provides detailed solutions and AI-powered analysis to help you identify weak areas, accuracy issues and time-management opportunities.

After an eligible mock-test attempt, the platform can analyse your performance and present insights such as topic-wise weak areas, time usage, accuracy and practical improvement guidance.

Study Playlist lets you organise YouTube study videos subject-wise so you can keep your learning resources together in a focused study workflow.

While watching a supported study video, Ask AI lets you ask questions about what you are learning and get explanations without leaving the study workflow.

Yes. Study Grid Prep includes Chat with AI for deeper explanations and ongoing questions related to your study topics and learning context.

The AI Quiz feature can generate a timed quiz from supported YouTube video content. You can choose quiz settings such as the number of questions, timer and marks per question.

Smart Focus Timer provides focused study sessions using a Pomodoro-style workflow. It is designed to help students build consistent study habits and track focused study time.

Yes. Smart Focus Timer includes live study rooms where students can study alongside others, interact through supported room features and take part in the platform's study community.

Todo Planner helps you plan daily tasks, set study goals, receive reminders and keep track of task completion as part of your preparation routine.

Performance Analysis can surface weekly study hours, XP progress, leaderboard history and subject-wise progress so you can understand how your preparation is moving over time.

XP and leaderboard features add a progress and consistency layer to studying. Your activity can contribute to visible progress and ranking within the Study Grid Prep experience.

Time-Bound Solver provides timed problem-solving practice intended to improve speed and decision-making during competitive-exam preparation.

Eligible mock tests can provide detailed solutions so students can review questions after attempting them and understand mistakes more effectively.

Use mock tests and performance analysis to identify weak areas, review solutions, practise timed questions and then use focused study sessions and planning tools to work on those topics.

Yes. Study Grid Prep provides progress-oriented features such as focused-study tracking, weekly study hours, XP and task completion insights.

A practical workflow is to use the Focus Timer for concentrated sessions, take targeted practice tests, review AI analysis, focus on weak areas and use the Todo Planner to decide what matters next.
